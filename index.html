01 · About

I like knowing how things work underneath.

I'm a Computer Science and Russian graduate of Portland State University. I do my best work in low-level code: decoding opcodes, managing memory and registers, and making hardware and software talk to each other.

Most of my recent projects are things I built to learn. I've written a 6502 CPU and NES emulator in Rust, Linux character device drivers in C, a GPU renderer with wgpu and a physics sandbox in Go. I'm also into cybersecurity, including penetration testing and malware reverse engineering.
